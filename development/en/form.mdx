## Setup Form Login

1. Prepare your Login Page:

   ```html
   <!DOCTYPE html>
   <html lang="ar">
   <head>
     <meta charset="UTF-8">
     <title>My Project</title>
     <style>
       body{
         display: flex;
         justify-content: center;
         align-items: center;
         min-height: 100svh;
       }
   
       h1{
         width: 100%;
         text-align: center;
       }
     </style>
   </head>
   <body>
     <h1>Welcome to my Website</h1>
   </body>
   </html>
   ```
2. Add a Placeholder element where you want your Form to be inserted and give it a unique id:

   ```html
   <div id="login-form"></div>
   ```
3. Add this snippet to your HTML code:

   ```html
     <script src="https://bawabah.app/app/login-form.js"></script>
     <script>
       startBawabah({
         element:"{plachilder-element-id}",
         appId: "{your-app-id}",
         callbackUrl: "{your-callback-url}"
       })
     </script>
   ```

   > Replace `{your-app-id}` with your appId and `{your-callback-url}` with your callback URL From [Create Your First App](/getting-started/en/quickstart#create-your-first-app) Step.
